The beta.7 four-tile overview shows (1) latest odometer and CURRENT month ODO difference, (2) litres refuelled this month, (3) actual booked expenditure this month, (4) this month's actual expenditure divided by observed odometer distance. Historical selection below does not affect the overview. Checkpoint baseline is latest reading before calendar start, terminal reading latest within period. Initial partial periods are labelled, never described as exact full-month mileage. Full vehicle expense per km from the date-stamped ODO ledger is distinct from sum of TripDist. Refer to ODOMETER_ALGORITHM.md for precise calculation and ODOMETER_BETA7_TEST_PLAN.md for HA checks.
