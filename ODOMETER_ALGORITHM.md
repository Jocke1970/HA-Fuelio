# Odometer algorithm — introduced in beta.7, retained in beta.8

Fuelio's OBD trip `StartOdo`/`EndOdo` and fuel-up `Odo (km)` become dated numeric checkpoints. No plate, VIN, trip route or individual checkpoint list is retained in the published HA snapshot. A trip may cross a calendar boundary: `StartDate` is used for `StartOdo` when present, and `EndDate` for `EndOdo`. Aggregate precision is one calendar day.

For a calendar month/year, find the **latest day strictly before period start**, use its highest checkpoint as baseline, then use the highest checkpoint on the **latest day inside the period** as the endpoint. The difference is observed calendar-allocated travel. Without a pre-period baseline, take the first day's lowest in-period reading and mark `partial_start` rather than claiming full-calendar coverage. Two readings are needed if both endpoints occur on the same day. No valid in-period reading, one insufficient endpoint or ODO rollback yields unavailable distance and unknown SEK/km. For the lifetime range use the first day's minimum and last day's maximum in the import.

`TripLog.TripDist` is separately summed as **logged-trip km** and used for the number of trips and mean recorded trip length, *never* added to the ODO difference. These two distances need not match: ODO can capture unlogged travel and a previous-month baseline may allocate an intervening distance to the following month. In the 2026-09-22 real HA test, logged-trip count/distance agreed with Fuelio while the observed ODO-month value was slightly different. This difference is **open for checkpoint-level audit**, not evidence that the trip-log import failed. Frequent OBD checkpoints reduce, but do not remove, calendar-boundary uncertainty.

**Booked expenditure/km** still uses fuel and non-fuel payments by transaction date divided by the observed ODO difference. **Estimated consumed-fuel/km** is a separate beta.8 model that integrates ODO intervals at the estimated consumption/price known *at the interval's start*. Neither is an exact midnight-to-midnight mileage or fuel-accrual measure. See [analytics schema](docs/analytics-schema.md) and [beta.8 validation](BETA8_VALIDATION.md). Automatic Google Drive download is not implemented.
